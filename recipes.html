★★★ STRAWBERRY CHEESECAKE ★★★

INGREDIENTS:
    • Graham crackers
    • Melted butter
    • Cream Cheese
    • Salt
    • Eggs
    • Sour Cream
    • Vanilla extract
    • Fresh strawberries
HOW TO MAKE IT:
    1. Preheat the oven to 325°F.
    2.Crush the graham crackers and mix them with melted butter.
    3. Press the mixture into the bottom of the pan to make the crust.
    4. Add the eggs, sour cream, and vanilla.
    5. Pour the cheesecake filling over the crust.
    6. Bake until the cheesecake is set.
    7. Let it cool completely, then refrigerate.
    8. Make the strawberry topping and spread it on top.

~~~ FUDGY BROWNIES ~~~

INGREDIENTS:
  • ½ cup melted butter
  • cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • ⅓ cup cocoa powder
  • ½ cup flour
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ½ cup chocolate chips
    • HOW TO MAKE IT:
      1. Mix melted butter and sugar.
      2. Add eggs and vanilla.
      3. Mix in cocoa powder, flour, and salt.
      4.Fold in chocolate chips.
      5. Fold in chocolate chips.
      6. Pour into an 8x8 pan.
      7.Bake for 20-25 minutes.
      Cool, cut, and enjoy!

    ♥ RED VELVET COOKIES ♥

    INGREDIENTS:
      • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
      • ¼ cup cocoa powder
      • ½ cup butter
      • ¾ cup sugar
      • 1 egg
      • 1 tsp vanilla
      • Red food coloring
      • Chocolate chips (optional)
    HOW TO MAKE IT:
      1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
      2. Mix the butter and sugar.
      3. Add the egg, vanilla, and red food coloring.
      4. Mix in the flour, cocoa powder, and baking soda.
      5. Fold in chocolate chips if desired.
      6. Scoop the dough onto a baking sheet.
      7. Bake for about 10-12 minutes.
      8. Let them cool and enjoy!
